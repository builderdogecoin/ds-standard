# Code of Conduct

The Doge Soft Orbit runs on good vibes and respect. Much kindness, very welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone was new to Dogecoin once.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- Assume good faith and help people find the answer.
- Keep it on topic and keep it legal.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats.
- Scams, phishing, impersonating Doge Soft or its team, or asking anyone for a seed phrase or password.
- Shilling, spam, or promoting unrelated projects.
- Posting someone's personal information.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, or block accounts that break these rules. Report problems directly to the maintainers at koinu@dogesoft.io.

This applies in this repo's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents Doge Soft.
